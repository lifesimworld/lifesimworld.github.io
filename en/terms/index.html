1. What LifeSim is

LifeSim is a free life simulation game in which you are born at random as one of the babies being born today and live that one life to its end. No account or sign-up is needed.

2. How the simulation works

The odds in the game are based on public statistics from bodies such as the UN, WHO and World Bank (sources are listed at the bottom of the page). Every life, however, is a random simulation: it does not describe a real person, it is not a prediction, and it is not medical, financial, legal or any other advice.

3. Content and age

LifeSim includes hard topics such as death, violence, illness, addiction and suicide at their real rates, so it is designed for ages 15 and up. No scene is shown, and you can leave out frightening scenes from the first screen. If you're struggling, the "If you're struggling" link on the first screen shows where to find help.
