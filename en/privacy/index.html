Privacy policy

In short: LifeSim collects no personal data from you. There is no account, sign-up, advertising, cookie or tracking. Your saves stay on your own device and never reach us.

Effective date: November 8, 2026 · This policy covers the LifeSim website (lifesimworld.github.io) and the LifeSim Android app.

1. Data we collect

None. LifeSim does not ask for or receive your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers. The game has no analytics, statistics or advertising tools, and nothing about the lives you play is sent to us.

2. What is stored on your device

So the game can pick up where you left off, some information is kept only on your device, in the browser's or the app's own storage (localStorage):

  • The life you are playing and records of your past lives
  • Your settings, such as language, theme, speed and sound

This information does not leave your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time (section 5).

3. Internet connections

Android app: All of the game's files are inside the app. The game works offline and connects to no server while you play.

Website: The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open it, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to run and secure the service, under its own rules: GitHub General Privacy Statement. This information does not reach us.

Sharing and outside links: When you share a life, the text or image goes straight to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, X or Threads); we cannot see it. Outside links in the game (for example to charities) open in your browser, and those sites' own privacy rules apply.

4. Children

LifeSim includes hard topics such as death, violence and illness at their real rates, so it is designed for ages 15 and up. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we collect none from children either.

5. Deleting your saves

  • Android: Settings → Apps → LifeSim → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Web: Clear this site's data in your browser settings (site data / cookies and site data).

Since your saves exist only on your device, deleting them removes them completely; we keep no copy.

6. Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. The saves on your device are entirely under your control.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new text on this page with a new effective date. If a feature that collects data is ever added to LifeSim, this page will be updated before that feature starts working.

8. Contact

You can send questions through LifeSim's GitHub page.
